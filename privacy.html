Privacy Policy

VOLEN Uploader · Effective 27 October 2026

Who uses this app

VOLEN Uploader is used only by the owner of the VOLEN YouTube channel to upload their own videos to that channel. It is not available to the public.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • The connected channel's name and ID, to confirm that the right channel is connected.
  • The videos the owner uploads through the app, with the title, description, privacy setting and publish time the owner gives them.

The app does not access comments, subscribers, viewers, analytics about individual people, or any other channel. It does not use cookies or tracking.

How the data is stored and shared

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a user environment variable. It is not written to files, logs or any server.
  • No data is sold, shared with or sent to any third party. Data goes only to Google's YouTube API.
  • Upload records (video ID, title, schedule time) are kept on the owner's computer for as long as the owner keeps the channel's publishing records, and can be deleted by the owner at any time.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (Third-party apps with account access). The owner can also delete the stored token from their computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
