About VOLEN Uploader

VOLEN Uploader is a small private tool used only by the owner of the VOLEN YouTube channel. It uploads videos that the owner recorded and edited from their own gameplay to that one channel, and schedules when they go public.

  • It is not offered to other users and has no sign-up.
  • It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos, set their title, description and publish time, and read back the channel name to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or other channels.
